Former Postdocs

Renata Magalhães Pirani

UCLA Chancellor’s Postdoctoral Fellow, 2023–2025
Now Postdoctoral Researcher, Vale Institute of Technology, Brazil
website
Download CV

Renata studies how historical and ecological processes generated the diversity of Neotropical amphibians and reptiles, combining genomics, phylogeography, and comparative methods. In the lab she asked how microhabitat and body size shape amphibian speciation rates across biogeographic scales, and used genomic tools to predict climate adaptation and vulnerability in the Atlantic Forest treefrog Dendropsophus elegans.
